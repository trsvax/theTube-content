A penny for your thoughts? That's not even close. A penny buys a million of them.

A 1TB SSD: $80. That's $0.00000008 per KB. One edit is ~100 bytes. Cost of logging one edit: $0.000000008. Eight zeros after the decimal.

A million edits for less than a penny.

The git history of a blog post — every word choice, every revision, the thinking process captured as commits — costs nothing. 104 posts with full history: 6MB. Less than one photo from your phone.

So why wouldn't you log everything? Every thought, every edit, every dead end. The storage is free. The history is the documentation. `git log` is the journey block.
