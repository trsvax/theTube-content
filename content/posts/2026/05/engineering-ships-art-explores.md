Engineering ships. Art explores. This project ships while exploring.

The blog works. A dollar a month. Solved. You could stop here. But the interesting part isn't the blog — it's the exploration of what's possible when you refuse the standard abstractions.

The tube doesn't need to exist for the blog to work. The WebDAV filesystem doesn't need to exist. The posts about the architecture don't need to exist. But they make the argument visible.

Nothing is done because done isn't the point. The point is the next thought.
