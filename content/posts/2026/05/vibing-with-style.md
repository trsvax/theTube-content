Vibe coding: you steer, the AI types. You describe what you want, it writes the code. The output is software but the process is conversation.

This is the same thing applied to writing.

I don't sit down to write a post. I think out loud into a chat. Half-formed ideas, typos, questions I'm asking myself. The AI catches the good bits — the phrasing that landed, the insight that just crystallized — and puts them in the right place. Structures them. Finds the thread.

The post is the artifact of the thinking, not the goal.

## How it works

I open a draft. I start talking. Sometimes I'm arguing with myself about a design decision. Sometimes I'm realizing something mid-sentence. The AI is listening for the moments where the thought sharpens — where the vague becomes specific — and writing those down.

"Are there two trust levels or one?" → I talk through it → the answer emerges → it's in the post.

"Why didn't I just use Cognito?" → I explain the constraint → the explanation is better than anything I'd have drafted cold → it goes in.

The conversation *is* the first draft. The AI is the editor who's in the room while you think.

## Not writing, not not-writing

It's not dictation — I'm not speaking finished sentences. It's not editing — there's no draft to start from. It's not prompting — I'm not describing what I want the AI to produce.

It's falling through ideas and the AI gives it style on the way down.

Vibe coding works because the programmer knows what the code should *do* even if they don't want to type it. Vibe writing works because the writer knows what they *think* even if they haven't organized it yet. The AI handles the organization. You handle the thinking.

## The post you're reading

This post was written that way. I was reviewing a different post, started asking questions about it, realized the answers were more interesting than the original draft, and the AI caught them as they fell. Twenty minutes of thinking out loud, structured into something readable.

I didn't plan this post. I didn't outline it. I fell through it with style.
